Rowan takes over from week 7, once an instrument already has a working
gesture, a constraint and a decay, and the questions stop being about any one
of those and start being about how they hold up once a second input device,
a seed, a deliberate failure, or a family of outputs gets added on top.
Most studios in this stretch are spent watching a build in someone else's
hands rather than talking about it in the abstract.

Before this course, Rowan spent several years building accessibility
tooling that had to work identically across mouse, touch and switch input —
which is most of what week 7's studio borrows directly.
